About Nairobi

Nairobi is the capital city of Kenya and one of the largest cities in East Africa. It serves as the country's economic, political and cultural hub

The city is home to nairobi National park, the only national park located within a capital city.
You can see lions and giraffes with skyscrappers in the backgroud.

Nairobi's tech scene has earned it the nickname "silicone Savannah.' Major tech companies and startups operate from hubs like ihub and Nairobi Garage.

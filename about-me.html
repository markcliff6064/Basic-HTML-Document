About me"

Hi there! My name is Amina Wanjiku. I am a self-taught software developer based in Nairobi, Kenya. I grew up in Nakuru and moved to Nairobi after finishing my diploma to pursue a career in tech.

I am currently learning full-stack web development at Mctaba Academy. I am passionate about building tools that solve real problems for people in East Africa, whether that is a better way to manage a mama mboga's inventory or a simpler interface for sending M-Pesa payments.

When I am not coding, you will probably find me at a local coffee shop sipping on a flat white, arguing about football, or planning my next hiking trip.
